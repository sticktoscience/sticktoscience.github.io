Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What this app is

Stick to Science Pipeline is a private tool used only by the owner of the Stick to Science YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

Through YouTube API Services, the app accesses the owner's own channel information, video metadata and YouTube Analytics data, and uploads videos to the owner's own channel.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to manage the channel and plan future content. It is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party.

Third-party terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data handling by Google is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Locally stored data is deleted on request.
